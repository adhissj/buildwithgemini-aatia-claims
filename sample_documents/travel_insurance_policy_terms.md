# AATIA Travel & Mobility Insurance Policy Terms (Ref: POL-TRV-2026)

## 1. Overview & Eligibility
AATIA Travel Insurance provides 24/7 global protection for retail and business travelers. Coverage applies automatically upon booking travel with a participating partner bank account or credit card.

## 2. Schedule of Benefits & Limits
* **Emergency Medical & Dental**: Up to $50,000 per trip ($100 flat deductible; $1,000 max sub-limit for emergency dental).
* **Trip Delay & Cancellation**: Up to $2,500 for delays exceeding 6 consecutive hours ($50/hour stipend).
* **Baggage & Personal Effects Loss**: Up to $3,000 total ($250 per item sub-limit; $100 deductible).
* **Rental Car Collision Damage Waiver (CDW)**: Up to $5,000 excess reimbursement.

## 3. Mandatory Claim Documents
To file a valid travel claim, policyholders must submit:
1. Itemized clinic/hospital or airline expense receipt (*Mandatory).
2. Proof of travel itinerary or airline boarding pass (*Mandatory).
3. Police or airline irregularity report (for theft or lost baggage claims).

## 4. Key Exclusions
* Claims arising from undeclared pre-existing medical conditions.
* Unattended baggage left in public areas.
* Extreme sports or high-risk activities without an active policy rider.
