# AATIA SME Commercial Stock Spoilage Protection Terms (Ref: POL-SME-2026)

## 1. Scope & Business Eligibility
AATIA Commercial Spoilage Protection is tailored for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs), artisan bakeries, restaurants, cold-chain logistics, and pharmaceutical retailers.

## 2. Coverage Scope & Trigger Event
Perishable inventory, refrigerated food stock, vaccines, or temperature-sensitive goods lost due to:
* Unplanned electrical power grid outage exceeding 4 consecutive hours.
* On-site refrigeration compressor failure or mechanical breakdown.

## 3. Payout Formula & Deductible
* **Deductible**: $500 flat deductible per incident.
* **Co-Insurance Coverage Rate**: 90% reimbursement of certified net inventory replacement cost.
* **Maximum Claim Limit**: $25,000 per policy year.

$$\text{Approved Payout} = (\text{Wholesale Inventory Loss Value} - \$500.00) \times 0.90$$

## 4. Mandatory Claim Documents
1. Certified supplier wholesale stock inventory manifest (*Mandatory).
2. Electric utility power outage incident report or technician repair invoice (*Mandatory).
3. Temperature log or disposal photo records.

## 5. Key Exclusions
* Spoilage resulting from deliberate power disconnection or unpaid utility bills.
* Loss caused by employee dishonesty, theft, or mold prior to power outage.
