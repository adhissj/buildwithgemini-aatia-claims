# AATIA Gadget & Personal Electronics Protection Terms (Ref: POL-GDT-2026)

## 1. Coverage Scope
AATIA Gadget Protection covers smartphone mobile devices, laptops, tablets, smartwatches, and DSLR camera gear against accidental damage, screen cracking, liquid spills, and electrical surge.

## 2. Payout Formula & Limits
* **Deductible**: $50 flat deductible per claim.
* **Co-Insurance Rate**: 80% reimbursement of approved net repair quote after deductible.
* **Maximum Claim Cap**: $1,500 per device per calendar year.

$$\text{Approved Payout} = (\text{Itemized Repair Quote} - \$50.00) \times 0.80$$

## 3. Mandatory Claim Documents
1. Official repair center invoice or diagnostic quote (*Mandatory).
2. Device serial number / IMEI verification photo or receipt (*Mandatory).

## 4. Key Exclusions
* Cosmetic scratches or wear-and-tear that do not impair primary device operation.
* Loss or theft without a formal police report filed within 48 hours.
