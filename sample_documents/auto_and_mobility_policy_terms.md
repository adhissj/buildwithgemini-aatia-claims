# AATIA Auto & Mobility Protection Terms (Ref: POL-AUT-2026)

## 1. Coverage Scope
AATIA Auto & Mobility Protection covers personal passenger motor vehicles, electric vehicles (EV), e-scooters, and micro-mobility rentals against collision damage, windshield cracks, emergency roadside towing, and battery failure.

## 2. Benefits & Deductibles
* **Windshield & Glass Replacement**: $50 deductible; 100% coverage up to $800.
* **Collision & Bodywork**: $200 flat deductible; 85% reimbursement after deductible.
* **Roadside Towing & EV Assistance**: $0 deductible; 100% reimbursement up to $300 per dispatch.

## 3. Mandatory Claim Documents
1. Certified auto body shop or mechanic invoice (*Mandatory).
2. Photos of vehicle damage with license plate visible (*Mandatory).
3. Police accident report (for multi-vehicle incidents).
